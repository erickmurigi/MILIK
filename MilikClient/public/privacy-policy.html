Overview

Milik is a business management platform operated by Milik Systems ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the Milik web platform or mobile application, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using the Milik platform, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Short version: We collect only what we need to operate the platform. We do not sell your personal data. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated as you use the platform.

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Account information Name, email address, password (hashed) Authentication and account management
Business data Property details, tenant records, lease terms, payment records, invoices Core platform functionality — entered by you
Device & usage data Device type, OS version, app version, session activity, error logs Performance monitoring, bug fixing, security
Push notification token Device push token (Expo/FCM) Sending in-app notifications you opt into
Financial transaction data M-Pesa transaction IDs, payment references Recording and payment reconciliation

We do not collect payment card numbers. M-Pesa payments are processed directly by Safaricom; we receive only the transaction confirmation details.

How We Use Your Information

  • Providing & operating the platform
  • User authentication
  • Sending invoices & payment receipts
  • Push notifications (opt-in)
  • Generating financial reports
  • Customer support
  • Security & fraud prevention
  • Legal compliance

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

PartyWhy
Safaricom (M-Pesa / Daraja API) Processing M-Pesa C2B payments and STK push
Africa's Talking Sending SMS notifications to tenants and payers
Expo / Google Firebase (FCM) Delivering push notifications to the mobile app

Legal authorities When required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights

Security

We take security seriously and implement the following safeguards:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit
  • Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes
  • JWT tokens with expiry & rotation
  • Role-based access control
  • Input validation & XSS protection
  • Database access restricted by IP allowlist

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at info@milikproperty.com.

Data Retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 90 days, unless we are required to retain it longer by law (for example, financial records required for tax compliance).

Anonymised or aggregated data that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely for analytics purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of push notifications
  • Withdraw consent

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@milikproperty.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

The Milik web platform uses session cookies strictly necessary for authentication. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

The Milik mobile app stores authentication tokens in device secure storage (AsyncStorage) — no browser cookies are used in the app.

Children's Privacy

Milik is a business platform intended for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or in-app notification. Your continued use of the platform after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
